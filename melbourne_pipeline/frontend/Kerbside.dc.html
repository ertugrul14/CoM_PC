Step 07 · Data cube assembly

Assemble the data cube.

Every 15 minutes the sensor network wakes. Pedestrian gates count footfall. Parking bays log occupancy. Weather stations record conditions. Each reading is snapped to its street and written into one column. 14,400 columns later, five months of the city are frozen in a single cube.
